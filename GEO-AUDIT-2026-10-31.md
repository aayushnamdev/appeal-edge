## Findings

### Critical
1. Homepage carried figures outside the confirmed list: 300+ accounts reinstated, 98% pre scan accuracy, $340,000 and 38 hours case study, 5+ years. **Resolution:** Likith (Head of Operations) confirmed 300+, 5+ years and the case study. The 98% claim is removed.
2. Regions did not match across pages. **Resolution:** seven regions everywhere (US, UK, EU, Canada, Mexico, Australia, India). Japan removed.

### High
3. No "not legal advice" disclaimer anywhere.
4. `llms.txt` linked only `/blog/`. No `llms-full.txt`.
5. 40 of 60 posts credited to the generic "Appeal Edge" byline.
6. Organization `sameAs` held only Instagram and LinkedIn. Person nodes carried name, title and employer only.
7. One shared `og-image.png` on all 60 posts.
8. No Bing signal: no IndexNow key file.
9. Service pages open with label headings, not question headings with direct answers.

### Medium
10. HowTo markup on 10 posts. HowTo has produced no rich results since 2023.
11. No `speakable` markup.
12. Only 9 of 60 posts show an "Updated" date on the page.
13. `/amazon-seller-of-record-explained/` title competes with two dropshipping posts.
14. 42 posts have no in body link to a service page.
15. No hreflang between the US and India service pages.
16. Apex redirect resolves as a 307 (temporary) set at the Vercel domain level.
17. HSTS lacks `includeSubDomains`.

### Low
18. `/experts/` returns a 200 stub with a meta refresh, not a server side 301.
19. About 23MB of unused images tracked in git.
20. Service pages are thin (632 to 841 words) with no comparison table.
