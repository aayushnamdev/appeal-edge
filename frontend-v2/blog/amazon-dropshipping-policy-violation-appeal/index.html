Dropship Policy

Amazon dropshipping policy violation: what Amazon actually prohibits and how to appeal.

Amazon's dropshipping policy isn't a blanket ban. It's a specific requirement about who the customer sees as the seller. Here's the actual violation that triggers suspension, and what a compliant fulfillment process looks like.

Khushi Narwal, COO · Published July 25, 2026 · 6 min read

Dropshipping is allowed on Amazon. What isn't allowed is obscuring who the actual seller is. That distinction is the entire policy, and misunderstanding it is what leads most sellers into a suspension they didn't see coming.

What Amazon's dropshipping policy actually allows

Amazon's dropshipping policy permits a seller to fulfill orders through a third party as long as that seller is the seller of record, is identified as such on every packing slip and invoice, and handles customer returns themselves. The customer needs to experience the transaction as coming from you, regardless of who physically ships the product.

The specific violation that triggers suspension

The policy violation Amazon actually enforces against is buying a product from another retailer and having it shipped directly to your customer with that retailer's name, invoice, or branding visible on the package. This exposes the real source of the product and breaks the seller of record requirement, which is what triggers enforcement, not the practice of using a third party fulfillment source in general.

Buying from another marketplace retailer and shipping direct is the common trap

Sourcing inventory by purchasing it from another online retailer and having that retailer ship straight to your customer almost always exposes the other retailer's branding somewhere in the package, whether on the invoice, the shipping label, or a promotional insert. That exposure is what Amazon's policy specifically targets.

What the appeal needs to show

The plan needs a corrected fulfillment process: either your own branded packing slips and invoices, or a switch to a compliant third party logistics provider or FBA, along with evidence the change has actually been made. A plan that describes the old process without showing what's different going forward doesn't give Amazon anything concrete to act on.

Frequently asked questions

Is dropshipping allowed on Amazon?

Yes, but only if you are the seller of record, identified as such on all packing slips and invoices, and you handle customer returns yourself.

What is the specific dropshipping violation Amazon suspends accounts for?

Buying products from another retailer and having them shipped directly to the customer with that retailer's information visible on the packaging or invoice.

Can I use a third party fulfillment service and stay compliant?

Yes, as long as the packing slips and invoices identify you as the seller and the fulfillment provider doesn't expose its own retail branding to the customer.

What does a dropshipping violation appeal need to include?

A corrected fulfillment process, evidence the change has been made, and confirmation that packing slips and invoices now identify you as the seller of record.
