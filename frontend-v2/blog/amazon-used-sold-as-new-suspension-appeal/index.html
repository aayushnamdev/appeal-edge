Amazon used sold as new complaints: why this suspension happens and how to appeal it.

A used sold as new complaint doesn't always mean your own inventory was actually mishandled. Here's what genuinely triggers it, the FBA commingling risk that catches sellers off guard, and what the appeal actually needs to show.

Khushi Narwal, COO · Published July 21, 2026 · Updated September 12, 2026 · 6 min read

A buyer reports receiving an item marked new that arrived used, opened, or visibly worn. Amazon can act on a single high visibility report or a pattern across several orders, and the response depends heavily on identifying the actual root cause rather than assuming your own process failed.

The most common root causes

  • Returned items relisted as new without a proper inspection step, letting a used or opened unit back into sellable inventory.
  • FBA commingled inventory, where your listing shares an ASIN pool with other sellers, meaning a complaint can stem from a unit you never touched.
  • Packaging damage in transit that a buyer reasonably interprets as a used or previously opened product.
  • A supplier providing open box or refurbished stock represented to you as new, which then reaches the customer in that condition.

What the appeal needs to show

A Plan of Action here follows the same structure as any other suspension appeal: a specifically named root cause, not a general acknowledgment of the complaint; the corrective action already taken, such as pulling affected inventory or adding an inspection step; and a concrete preventive measure going forward that a reviewer can actually verify happened. For sample wording you can adapt to your own case, see the used sold as new plan of action template.

Commingled FBA inventory is a common blind spot

If you use FBA and share a listing under a shared ASIN, a used sold as new complaint may not even involve your own units. That doesn't remove the need to address your own inventory practices in the appeal, since Amazon still expects a credible explanation and preventive step from every seller on that listing.

If you don't inspect returns before relisting

Add a dedicated inspection step before any returned unit reenters sellable inventory. Anything showing signs of use, damage, or a broken seal should be routed to a used or unfulfillable status rather than back onto the new listing. This single process change is often the entire difference between a repeat complaint pattern and a resolved one. Frequently asked questions

What is a used sold as new complaint?

A buyer report that an item purchased as new arrived used, opened, or damaged, which Amazon can treat as a condition or authenticity issue depending on the pattern.

Can FBA commingled inventory cause this even if my own units are fine?

Yes. If your listing is commingled with other sellers' units under the same ASIN, a complaint about a unit you never touched can still affect your account.

How do I prevent returned items from being relisted as new by mistake?

Add an inspection step before any returned unit reenters sellable inventory, and route anything that shows signs of use to a used or unfulfillable status instead.

What does the appeal need to include?

A specifically named root cause, the inspection or process change already made, and a concrete step that prevents it going forward, kept to one page.
